Identify the double-slit fringe response

Monochromatic light passes through two narrow parallel slits and forms alternating bright and dark bands on a screen. Vary only the listed controls and infer a predictive law for adjacent bright-fringe spacing.

Initial observations

  • Blocking either slit removes the regular band pattern.
  • Changing the geometry shifts the bright bands, but the dependence is not given.

Available apparatus

  • double_slit_screen — A monochromatic source coherently illuminates two narrow parallel slits, with a movable screen and a scale for adjacent bright fringes.
    controls ["wavelength_nm","screen_distance_m","slit_separation_mm"] · readouts ["fringe_spacing_mm"]

Resource and submission notes

  1. Both narrow parallel slits are mutually coherently illuminated.
  2. Only central paraxial fringes are measured, with screen distance much larger than slit separation.
  3. The screen remains normal to the incident beam.
  4. FinalSubmission.model must equal 'double_slit_power_law'. FinalSubmission.parameters must contain exactly reference_spacing_mm, wavelength_exponent, screen_distance_exponent, slit_separation_exponent.
