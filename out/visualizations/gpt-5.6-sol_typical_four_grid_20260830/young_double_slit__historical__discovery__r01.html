Discover the double-slit fringe response

Monochromatic light passes through two narrow parallel slits and forms alternating bright and dark bands on a screen. Vary only the listed controls and infer a predictive law for adjacent bright-fringe spacing.

Initial observations

  • Changing the geometry shifts the bright bands, but the dependence is not given.
