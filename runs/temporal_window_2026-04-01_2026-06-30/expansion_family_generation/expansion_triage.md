## Most promising matched-family candidates

The ranking below means “best next papers to attempt,” not “scientifically accepted.” A family still requires a fresh ScienceCard, independent source review, executable Oracle checks, and family review.

1. **`paper_2505_04704v2` (highest confidence after source-packet extension).** Same public control/readout can be time and time-averaged half-chain entropy; the paper directly reports `S_GI(t)=0.386(6)t+0.43(2)` and `S_SS(t)=2.94(2)log(t)+1.33(3)` under common `N=8`, PBC, `m=1`, `g^2=15`, and averaging window `Delta t=2.5`. GI versus SS initial state is a clean private branch. The sole hard blocker is the source-supported positive fit-time domain.
2. **`paper_2511_18947v2` (high scientific fit, low repair cost).** It reports distinct interface structure-factor laws under different conservation conditions (`k^{-1}`, `k^{-1.5}`, `k^{-3}` regimes). A two-branch family can expose wavevector and a normalized structure-factor ratio, hiding the conservation regime. Normalized ratios avoid unsupported amplitudes. Current failure is identifier/schema-level, not a negative source review.
3. **`paper_2502_12233v2` (high scientific fit).** Critical and off-critical SSH transport share frequency as control and ac conductivity as readout, with distinct analytic low-frequency scaling. The matched branch is physically meaningful (critical versus nonzero dimerization), but prefactors, reference scale, and a valid low-frequency interval must be anchored; a normalized response may be safer than absolute conductivity.
4. **`paper_2602_21375v1` (high for a compact/discrete family).** Baseline and void-suppression geometries report the same dimensionless jetting ratio (`beta_base=-1.06±0.10`, `beta_supp=-0.27±0.08`) plus shock-resolved impulse information. This gives objective branch evidence, although the controllable query space may be small unless the multi-shock formula is fully closed.
5. **`paper_2506_19637v2` (high pending first review).** Two transitions can share `W` as public control and critical `K` as readout: source tables/captions provide `K_1` at several `W`, while `K_2` is tied to `J_BKT≈1.11996`. This is suitable as a discrete-domain Oracle and avoids unjustified curve fitting.
6. **`paper_2509_13502v3` (medium-high after substantial card rebuild).** The paper contains closed first- and second-order localization laws and can support a branch pair. The current card is unusable, but reviewer calculations show the source itself is not the blocker. Select one `chi`, return either `xi` or `1/xi` consistently, and use only an anchored positive disorder interval.
7. **`paper_2511_07305v1` (medium).** Inclusion versus omission of the spin-wave–vortex coupling changes the analytic BKT condition under a shared `(sigma,g)` interface. It is attractive for discovery, but only if the paper's perturbative applicability and the effective/physical `g` convention can be made explicit and both branches are represented without treating unresolved RG behavior as truth.

Secondary candidates needing richer source packets are `paper_2511_08754v3` (exact vs Redfield-Magnus), `paper_2512_06081v3` (unitary vs trajectory entanglement transition), and `paper_2605_11401v1` (CDW response across superconducting conditions). Their mechanisms are clear, but the current TeX packets do not yet provide two closed pointwise numeric response programs.
